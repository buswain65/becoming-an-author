Chapter 2: The Summoning Chamber

This is the darkest chapter in the book. The trick is the tone plan from Stage 5: let the horror be real, and let Evan’s deadpan be how he copes, never a joke about the pain itself.

Here’s a draft scene list, built from your plan:


| Scene | What happens | Ends on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Still a dream? | Evan looks around: the stone room, robed figures, and the arching marks on the floor that look like inlaid metal but aren’t shiny, so he decides he’s imagining things. His isekai brain wakes up: was I summoned? Where’s my status screen? | Hope, briefly |
| 2. The verdict | Loric Pryce and the summoners examine him and find no abilities at all. Their frustration turns to contempt: he must have been so far below normal that his power only brought him up to their level. Garrick stands by, uneasy. | “It could be worse” gets worse |
| 3. Mr. Pliers | The torture begins, out of frustration. A beat of real pain and fear first, then Evan’s bathroom callback: at least he doesn’t need to go anymore. | Things go dark |
| 4. Disposal | They decide he’s worthless and kill him, or think they do. Garrick is ordered to get rid of the body. | Water, darkness, a voice |
