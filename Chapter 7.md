# Chapter 7

The next morning, I was eating breakfast in actual clothes for the first time in two weeks when Plan B came by the chapel.

"Have you had your meeting with the Guild yet?" Vagid asked.

"No. I figure they just forgot, so I was going to go there today. I just don't know where it is."

"Typical," Orrin sighed.

"We had the same thought," said Vagid. "We can take you, and give our report while we're there."

"Let's get going," Vagid said, heading out of the chapel.

This was my first real look at the town center: timber and whitewash, people nodding to Plan B as we passed. Between two rooftops, I caught a glimpse of the river glinting in the sun.

"The Wendway," Nara said, when she saw me looking. "It runs all the way down from the mountains."

In the center of town, Vagid led us into a two-story building that looked like an Old West saloon someone had dropped into a fantasy novel.

*Howdy, pardner!*

I expected the inside to have a bar. Instead, it looked like a bank. A long counter ran the length of the room, with a divider every few feet to separate the receptionists' windows. Lines were painted on the floor to show where each line should start.

*It wasn't a saloon. It was the DMV.*

There were three receptionists, and the lines in front of them varied in length. I figured Vagid would pick the shortest one. Instead, he headed straight for the longest.

"Wouldn't the shorter line be faster?" I asked.

"Not necessarily," he replied.

"Vagid has a thing for Mara," Orrin whispered.

"If by 'thing' you mean respect, then yes. I do," Vagid said dryly.

As we waited in line, I watched Mara handle the adventurers ahead of us. She was young, pretty, and very perky. She was exactly what every isekai I'd ever read had promised!

*This is where my adventure finally kicks in!*

The other two receptionists were older than Mara, and seemed more efficient, if less enthusiastic.

When we reached the front of the line, Mara beamed. "Hi, Vagid! It's so good to see you again!"

"Hi, Orrin! It's so good to see you again!" Mara said, beaming at him. "Hi, Nara! It's so good to see you again!"

*Okay. So she's just stuck in a loop.*

I glanced at Vagid to see if he'd caught the repeat. He hadn't. He was still glowing from his own greeting.

Mara turned to me. "Hi! It's so good to see you again!"

I looked at Nara, then at Orrin. *Again?*

"We were supposed to have a meeting with the Guild about the bodies-in-the-river job," said Vagid.

"Okay! Let me check on that," said Mara, and she started looking through a ledger. "You took that job a few weeks ago, so the meeting should have been sometime after that."

She flipped through the pages. Then: "Nope. Nothing on the books."

"Prue, do you know anything about a Guild meeting with Plan B?" Mara asked the nearer of the other two receptionists.

Prue wasn't as young as Mara, but she didn't look older than her early twenties. She wore her waist-length auburn hair in a braid. When she heard her name, she glanced over. "Why would I have heard about a meeting?"

"The Boss is in. Why not just have the meeting now?" Prue suggested.

"But he might be busy," Mara protested.

"That's his problem, not mine," said Prue, getting up from her window and motioning for us to follow her.

Prue led us upstairs to a set of double doors. Without knocking, she pushed one open. "Hey, Cutty! You need to meet with these guys!"

Behind a desk by the window sat a large chair, facing away from us. At Prue's words, the chair slowly turned around. In it sat a short, man-like being with small, round ears on top of his head.

*Oh my god. Is that a beastkin?*

He had the air of Dr. Evil from *Austin Powers*. All he was missing was Mr. Bigglesworth, the hairless cat.

"Do they have an appointment?" asked the beastkin.

*What is he? Not a cat. Maybe a mouse? Okay. GM Mouse.*

"They do now," said Prue. "This is Guild Master Cuthbert Mossley."

*Cuthbert. Of course. It couldn't be something cool, like Reepicheep.*

"Hello, Guild Master. My party and I are called Plan B," said Vagid. "We took the job looking into the bodies turning up in the farmers' nets."

Cuthbert turned, hauled a ledger nearly as big as he was off the shelf behind him, and set it down on his desk with a thump. He opened it and paged quickly through it.

"Ah. Yes. Here it is. You took this job just over two weeks ago, correct?"

"That is correct, Guild Master," Vagid confirmed.

Cuthbert tapped the page. "My ledger says that Plan B has only three members. Yet there are four of you in my office. Explain."

"We were investigating and found another body. But this one wasn't dead yet," Vagid explained. "This is the man we found. His name is Evan."

"You said body, but this man seems to be in good health," said Cuthbert.

"Yes. When we found him, he was seriously injured, so we took him to Amaranth's Chapel. I have a list of his injuries from Halden," said Vagid, handing Cuthbert a folded piece of paper.

*There's a list? Don't know. Don't want to know.*

Cuthbert unfolded the paper and took a few minutes to read it. Every few sentences, he looked up at me, as if to check that I was the same person.

Then he began reading the list aloud. "Broken fingers?"

He looked at me expectantly, eyebrows raised.

"Um. Rocks?"

"Skin taken from the arm?"

"Uh. Rocks?"

"Burns from red-hot pokers?"

"Um. Hot? Rocks?"

"Knife wounds?"

"Yeah, uh, sharp rocks."

"Club marks?"

"Yeah, that would be rocks too."

With each answer, Cuthbert dutifully wrote *rocks* in the ledger. As I answered, I glanced at the members of Plan B.

Their expressions all said the same thing: *You have some explaining to do.*

*So much for "close to the truth."*

Cuthbert wrote a little more in the ledger, mumbling to himself, "And Plan B… four members." Then he looked up. "Well. It looks like I can mark this job completed. Job well done. You can collect your reward at the counter downstairs," he said, pointing to the door.

I walked out, Plan B close behind me. Everyone was quiet until Vagid said, "We need to talk."

*Yeah. This is not going to be fun.*
