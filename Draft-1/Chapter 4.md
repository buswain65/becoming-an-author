# Chapter 4

I was disoriented, though I couldn't tell whether it was from talking with Tired Guy, the clubbing, or any of the many other pains I had. I managed to keep my feet downstream and slowly worked my way into an eddy with a sandy beach.

Lying there, ass in the sand and buck naked, I couldn't muster the strength to do anything but pass out.

*So much for not passing out.*

\* \* \*

Orrin saw him first.

Not that anyone would notice. Vagid was too busy arguing that Orrin should carry the pack, because Orrin was taller.

"You're shorter," Orrin pointed out. "Less distance for it to fall if you drop it."

"Which is why I'm in charge of things that matter," Vagid said, not missing a step. "You're in charge of things that are high up."

Behind them, Nara sighed. She'd been sighing for about a mile.

"Three days of walking this river," Orrin said, "and not one body. Remind me why we took this job?"

"Because the farmers downstream keep pulling dead people out of their nets," said Vagid, "and the Guild pays by the answer, not by the walk."

Then Orrin stopped. "Huh. Who decides to go swimming in the river at this time of year? Naked?"

"It wouldn't be my first choice," said Vagid.

Nara didn't laugh. "He's not swimming."

The banter died. Vagid looked at the body on the sand, then at the river. "Well," he said quietly. "Looks like we found what the Guild sent us for."

"It could be some kind of trap," Vagid warned. "Let's be careful. Orrin, get up in one of these trees and cover us. Nara, you and I will approach him. Have a spell ready in case we need a shield."

From his branch, Orrin heard Nara gasp. "Those wounds. What happened to him? Is he still alive?"

"I don't know. Use your magic. See if anyone's in the forest near us. If the people who did this come back, we don't want to be caught here."

Nara took a calming breath. "Search." She focused for a few seconds, and Orrin saw her eyes flick toward him as he slid down the trunk. The corner of her mouth twitched. She said nothing.

"Nothing within a league," she told Vagid. She sounded winded.

"Orrin!" Vagid yelled toward the trees. "Come on down. We need to patch this guy up and get him back to town before he kicks it."

But Orrin had already dropped out of the tree and slipped through the brush while they talked. He stepped out right beside Vagid. "Yeah, okay."

Vagid jumped. "I really wish you wouldn't do that."

Behind him, Nara pressed a hand over her mouth. Her shoulders were shaking.

Orrin crouched beside the stranger. "He's alive, anyway. Dead men don't bleed."

They bound the worst of the wounds with strips torn from Vagid's spare shirt, then got the stranger up onto Orrin's back and tied him in place with a length of rope.

As Vagid tightened the last knot, the stranger stirred and mumbled something, right by Orrin's ear, in a language Orrin had never heard.

"That's what I get for eating pizza."

"Do either of you know what language that was?" asked Vagid. "It wasn't Hallish, and it wasn't Dravian either. I don't recognize it."

"Never heard it before," said Orrin. "What's a *peet-sha* anyway?"

"Me neither," Nara agreed.

"Can one of you lend him a cloak or something?" Orrin griped. "I really don't want a naked guy flopping around on my back. The rumors would be brutal."

A short time later, the earthen walls of Millbrook came into view. The town constable waved as they approached.

"Who's this?" asked Baz.

"Your guess is as good as ours. We found him on the way back from that Guild job," Vagid replied. "The bodies in the river. Only this one's still breathing. We're taking him to Halden's."

"Well, I don't think he'll be stirring up much trouble," said Baz. "Not in his current condition, anyway."

"Can we hurry this up?" Orrin complained. "He's not the lightest person around. And he's still naked."

"Come on, then," said Nara. "See you later, Baz."

They went straight to Amaranth's Chapel.

"Hey, Halden!" Orrin yelled. "We need your help with this one."

A bear of a man came in through a back door and walked over to them. "I don't recognize him," said Halden.

"We didn't either," said Vagid. "But he was in trouble…"

"And naked," Orrin chimed in.

"Yes, and naked. So we thought we should help."

"He has a lot of wounds, and he's lost a lot of blood," said Halden. "It will take me several days to get him out of danger."

"Once we cash in these monster cores, we can help with the cost," said Vagid. "We didn't get much, but it should help."

Halden looked down at the stranger for a long moment. "Whoever did this to him," he said quietly, "knew exactly what they were doing."
