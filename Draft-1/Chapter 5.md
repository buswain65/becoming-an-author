# Chapter 5

It's a familiar dream. Katie is telling me, "I need to invest my time and energy into things that actually matter, and have a future."

I ask, "I don't matter, or I don't have a future?"

She answers, "I don't know… maybe both…"

The ache in my chest feels like an anchor, dragging me down.

Then I heard something odd.

"He's getting better, but he still hasn't woken up. Maybe we should toss a bucket of cold water on him?"

The voice was gruff and deep, but not unkind. I had no idea how I knew that.

They were close by. At the word *bucket*, every part of me tried to get away. I struggled to get one eye open, stared at what appeared to be a bear in a robe, and said, "Eep!"

Then darkness took me again.

I don't know how long I was out, but when I next opened my eyes, it was dark outside. Someone had left a candle burning on the bedside table. *That was thoughtful.*

I was still really tired, but at least I wasn't in the ritual room, or the river. "I have no idea where I am," I said slowly, "but it's a big improvement on where I was."

"That's a good way to look at it," came a man's voice from the other side of the bed.

I slowly turned my head until I could see who it was. An average-looking guy, late teens or early twenties, with blond hair. Not especially tall, either, considering his feet didn't touch the floor as he sat in his chair.

"My name is Vagid. My friends and I found you while we were out on a Guild job," he said. "You weren't in the best shape."

From somewhere else in the building, muffled by a door, someone called, "And naked."

"And naked," Vagid agreed.

"Yeah. Probably not my best showing," I joked. "I'm almost afraid to look at what was done to me."

Just then the door opened, and a huge man in a robe walked in. *So I hadn't dreamed the bear.*

*Okay. Bear Guy.*

"You healed much better than most would have," Bear Guy said, studying me a moment longer than seemed necessary. "Most of your injuries won't leave any permanent damage, other than scars. My name is Halden. I'm the cleric for this chapel."

"I'm Evan. Can you tell me where I am?"

"Of course. You're in the Sunhallow Kingdom, in the town of Millbrook," said Vagid. "You do know about the kingdom, right?"

Some stubborn part of me was still hoping the last few days had been a really bad dream. "I have no idea what you're talking about. How far is the Nantahala from here?"

Vagid and Halden exchanged a look. "We don't know what that is," said Halden.

I started to get out of bed, but for some reason the lights began to dim. I saw Halden raise a hand and say, loudly and clearly, "Light heal."

*Oh, yeah. I've been isekai'd.*

"Hmm. Usually people don't faint until they see the bill," said Halden. "You should get some more rest. We can talk about these things, and more, tomorrow."

"And my friends would like to be there too, if that's all right with you," said Vagid.

"Yeah. Works for me," I slurred, as the room went dim again.

\* \* \*

This time, it was brightness on my eyelids that brought me back. I had no idea what time it was, or what day, month or year. But the breeze across my skin felt amazing.

I wanted to talk to Tired Guy. The problem was, I'd spent my whole life not believing in gods, so I had no idea how you were supposed to reach one. *Is there a number? Do I get put on hold?*

Halden was a cleric. Talking to gods was literally his job. Maybe he could help, without my having to explain why I was asking.

I could finally sit up, even if I was still stuck in bed. *Celebrate the little things.*

I lifted the sheets and realized I wasn't naked anymore. Someone had put me in a robe, roughly the size of a tent. *Yay, clothes. At least now my dong isn't hanging out.*

I swung my legs over the side of the bed and gave myself a second to see if I was going to pass out again.

No dimming lights. No lightheadedness. *So far, so good.*

Now to see if I could still move around. Slowly, I put some weight on one leg. It immediately started shaking, like I was doing an Elvis impersonation. After a few seconds it calmed down enough that I could put more weight on it, and actually stand up.

*While hanging on to the headboard for dear life.*

Right as I was clinging to the headboard, about to try a step, the door opened and Halden walked in.

"It's good to see you motivated," he said, "but you'll still have to settle the bill before we can let you leave."

"About that. Everything I own is currently a robe, and it isn't mine," I said meekly.

"Don't worry about it. Plan B took care of it," Halden rumbled.

"Plan B? Is that some kind of code phrase?" I asked.

"Or something. The group that brought you in is an adventuring party known as Plan B. Vagid is their leader."

"I was wondering. You're a cleric, right?"

"I am."

"If I wanted to talk to a god, how would I go about that?"

"Hmm. We don't usually carry on conversations with gods. It's more of a one-way dialogue."

"Oh. Well, how would I go about that, then?"

"I usually just close my eyes, think about Amaranth, and tell him what I want to say."

"Wait. Who's Amaranth?"

At that moment, the door opened again. Vagid and two others stood in the doorway. They got a wonderful view of me in my tent-robe, clinging to the headboard.

*Awesome first impression.*

Halden turned to the group. "Did you drop him on his head or something?"

Vagid and the other two looked at each other. "Not while he was on *my* back," said the tall one.

Vagid looked slyly at the tall one and said, "Naked."

Tall Guy looked like he wanted to disappear, and the lady beside him snickered into her hand.

"It must have happened while I was in the river. I don't know how long or how far I came, but I remember a lot of rocks," I quickly added. *I don't know if I need to keep it a secret that I'm from a different world or not.*

"These are the friends I mentioned yesterday. This is Nara, and the abnormally tall one is Orrin."

"I'm only tall compared to you!" Orrin said. "Halden's taller than me. And even Naked Guy is the same height as me!"

*I'm not sure I can live that down.*

"Evan," I said, hoping to get away from that horrible nickname.

"Evan. Great to finally meet you!" Orrin said, a bit too enthusiastically, I thought.

"I, uh, just wanted to thank you all for paying for my healing. I don't have, um, well, anything right now. Not even clothes. But I can get some kind of work. It just might take a while before I can pay you back," I stammered.

"You don't owe us anything," Nara said. "We'd want someone to do the same for us."

I opened my mouth, then closed it again. I had no idea what to do with that.

"That reminds me," Vagid said. "The Guild wants to talk to you about what happened, once you're feeling better."

"Guild? Like an Adventurers' Guild?" I asked, hoping against hope.

"Yes. They coordinate any job outside the town, and even some inside," Nara chimed in. "We were on a job for them when we found you."

*Do they hand out rank cards? Is there a crystal that measures my mana?* Probably not. Knowing my luck, it was more like a temp agency with swords.

"Naked," added Orrin.

"Yeah, got it," I said quickly. *Man, I really need to work on getting rid of that nickname!*

"As much as I'd like to know why you were out there," Vagid said, "I think we should wait until you meet with the Guild. That way, you only have to tell the story once."

*Thank god! I really don't want to relive that. But maybe they can help me.*

"Yeah, that would be best," I said.

Vagid continued, "The Guild will send someone in the next day or two to make the arrangements, so just rest until then. We'll be leaving now. It's really good to see you doing better, Evan."

Plan B left, quietly closing the door behind them.

*I really need to talk to Tired Guy.*

"Halden, does the chapel have someplace quiet for prayer? And does it matter if I pray to a god other than your god?" I asked.

After pausing a moment, Halden said, "Amaranth is a very kind and patient god. But if you want to pray to a different god, they might find it better if you found someplace meaningful to them, and prayed there."

*Someplace meaningful to Tired Guy? I'm stumped.*

I asked Halden if I could sit somewhere quiet outside the chapel, and he agreed to help me out to a stone bench just beyond the front door.

"Thanks, Halden. I literally couldn't have done it without you!"

"I'll just add it to the tab."

*It's really hard to tell if he's joking.*

The bench was cold, and the sun was warm, and for a while I just sat there, being alive.

Then I tried it Halden's way. I closed my eyes and thought, as hard as I could, *Hello? Tired Guy? It's me. Evan. The naked one.*

Nothing. Not even hold music.

*Someplace meaningful.* What would mean anything to a god who quoted famous people and knew about Swiss cheese? I didn't even know what he was the god *of*.

Somewhere past the rooftops, I could hear water. Not loud. Just the low, steady rush of a river going about its business.

I opened my eyes.

The last time I'd talked to Tired Guy, I'd been floating down a river. He'd turned my feet downstream because I asked him to. *The past is set in stone. The future is like a river.*

*Huh.*

I knew where I needed to go. I just had to be able to walk there first.
