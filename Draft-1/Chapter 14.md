# Chapter 14

"Do you have any idea how long I've been yelling?" said Mister Soul Glow.

"Who are you?" I asked.

"I'm Xavier Monroe. I was summoned here from another world over two hundred years ago. Who are you?"

"Wow. I'm Evan McIntyre," I said.

"Hey, Evan. What are you talking about?" asked Vagid.

"Oh. Yeah. This is Xavier. He comes from my wo—home." I'd almost said "world" with Halden standing right there. I didn't know if he even knew about the summonings.

Vagid glanced at Xavier, then at Halden, and finally back at me. "What is he saying?"

"Hm. Xavier arrived in Sunhallow over two hundred years ago," I said. "I haven't had a chance to ask him much else."

When I said "two hundred years," Halden's eyes narrowed, and he looked at me. Nara seemed surprised. I couldn't see how Orrin reacted. He was still busy trying to stop my cut from bleeding into my eyes.

"How is it that only you understand him?" asked Halden, more calmly than I would have thought possible.

"We—came from the same village?" I said. It was the only thing I could think of. Halden's eyes stayed narrowed, and he kept watching me.

"Anyway, give me a few minutes to ask him why he's haunting this place," I said.

I turned back to Xavier. "So, Xavier, can you give me the elevator version of what happened to you?"

"Wow. It's been centuries since I heard that term. But, yeah," he said. "I came here to hide something. While I was here, the kingdom's assassins killed me. Now I'm stuck here."

"You're from Earth too," Xavier whispered.

"Yep. I've only been here for about a month, though," I said.

"What year was it on Earth when you were summoned?" asked Xavier.

I told him.

Xavier went very still. "That's impossible. That's the year I left."

*Two hundred years for him. The same year for me.* My head hurt, and not just from the rocks.

"Um. Evan, what did he say?" asked Vagid.

"So, Xavier was murdered here over two hundred years ago." I paused, trying to think of a good way to say it. "By assassins sent by the Sunhallow Kingdom."

"Why?" asked Halden. "Why murder him here?"

"He came here to hide some information," I said.

"What information?" asked Halden.

"I haven't asked him that yet."

"Xavier, what were you trying to hide?" I asked.

Xavier paused for a moment and looked at the members of Plan B, then at Halden, and finally back at me.

"Notes," he said.

"What were the notes about?"

"Everything. Everything I learned about the summoning. How it works. What it costs. The experimenting the kingdom has done. Everything I could find out."

I looked at my friends: the members of Plan B, and Halden. *I have to tell them the truth.*

After a deep breath, I said, "He hid his notes about the summoning ritual. Everything he could find out before he was killed."

I looked at Halden when I said it. His expression didn't change.

"What summoning ritual?" asked Halden.

I told Halden everything I'd told Plan B.

His expression didn't change.

"How do we know that you can actually talk to that thing?" asked Halden.

"I can ask him to do something, if you want," I said. "How about I ask him to go to the stairs? I won't point or turn toward them."

Halden nodded.

"Xavier, I need to prove to these people that we're actually talking and that you understand me. Is that okay?" I asked.

"Sure."

"Can you go over to the stairs, wait a second, then come back?" I asked.

"Okay." Xavier drifted over to the stairs, waited a moment, then came back to the center of the room.

For the first time, Halden's expression changed. No longer wary, but relieved.

I looked at my friends in Plan B. Orrin was staring at me like I'd grown an extra head again. Vagid was sighing and rubbing his face. Nara looked at me, then at Xavier, then back at me.

"It was true," said Vagid. "All the things you told us before. It was all true."

"Yeah. Unfortunately," I said.

"And you really are an Otherborn?" asked Orrin. "Without any magic?"

"Yeah. Also unfortunately."

"All those wounds you had. The kingdom did that to you? Why?" asked Nara.

"Yeah. I'd like to say it's because they're jerks, but that doesn't seem strong enough. It was because the summoning has a high cost, and they blamed me when it failed."

"Evan, I need to ask a favor of you," said Xavier. "I can't move on until my unfinished business is taken care of."

"The notes?" I asked.

"The notes. Yes."

"Okay. Has no one found them?" I asked.

"Not yet. But I can show you where they are."

I turned back to Plan B and Halden and said, "He needs us to take his notes. That's his unfinished business."

"What're you going to do with them?" asked Vagid.

"I'll give them to Tired Guy. Maybe they have some of the information he needs to fix the problem," I said.

I turned back to Xavier and told him everything I'd told Plan B. I needed him to understand that I was going to use the notes to try to fix things. I hoped that would help him pass on when the time came.

Xavier was quiet for a long moment. "Then it wasn't for nothing," he said.
