# Chapter 13

I turned toward the voice and saw him sitting at a corner table with a cup of tea and a stack of empty dishes. "What are you doing here, Halden?" I asked.

"I need to watch out for my investment. If you die, who will pay your tab?" Halden said, and raised an eyebrow.

I sighed. I still couldn't tell if he was joking or not.

"How did you get here before us?" I asked.

"Coach." He sipped his tea. "Took a day and a half."

"You rode," said Nara, giving me a pointed look.

"Yeah. Just cost two rays. Three if you wanted a cushion," said Halden.

"Just two rays," Nara said, glaring at me.

"Three days! We *walked* for three days!" said Orrin.

Vagid just shook his head and sighed.

"In my defense, I thought a coach would cost way more than that," I said.

"Then. You. Ask," said Nara, poking me with each word.

I turned back to Halden and said, "That explains how, but not why."

"Amaranth," Vagid said wearily.

"Yes," said Halden.

"Sorry. I'm not following," I said.

"The Hollin's Cross chapel is a chapel of Amaranth," said Halden.

We took our gear up to the room, then went back down and sat at Halden's table.

"Evan, when I told you that my communication with Amaranth was one way, it wasn't exactly true. Sometimes I get something along the lines of a feeling. Like I need to do something," said Halden. "I don't know if it's Amaranth telling me to do it, but I like to think it is."

"Okay," I said. *So why could Tired Guy talk to me when the other gods couldn't?*

"When you mentioned the Hollin's Cross chapel, I got the feeling that I needed to be there too. So, I'm here."

As simple as that.

While I was thinking it over, Mave walked up with a huge tray of food and said, "Dinner is served, my lovelies!"

"If Amaranth sent you that feeling, I wonder what he wants you to do," I said.

"She," said Halden.

"Huh?" I said.

"Amaranth is a goddess."

"Oh. Sorry about that," I said.

*I've been sleeping in her house for a month.*

We dug into the food Mave had brought: what looked like little cubes of grilled beef with vegetables, a small cup of gravy, a large loaf of bread, and ales for each of Plan B, with more tea for Halden.

We were still eating and planning our trip to the chapel when Mave walked by and stopped. A slight look of concern crossed her face, and she said, "You shouldn't go to that cursed place. It's too dangerous. None of the other adventurers have ever come back."

*Other adventurers? Prue said no one had taken this job.*

"Other adventurers?" said Vagid, echoing my thoughts.

"They come every year or two, all happy and full of themselves. They leave for that place and never return," said Mave. Then she moved on to another table.

"The Millbrook Guild isn't the only guild, and others may have the same job posted," said Halden.

We finished our meal and agreed to meet for breakfast, then set off for the chapel afterward.

I went out behind the inn and sat on the porch.

*I wish I could talk to Tired Guy,* I thought.

*You can,* came the reply, which startled me, since I wasn't near the river.

*Wait! I can talk to you anywhere?*

*Of course. Just not near other gods' houses.*

I probably would have figured that out if I'd thought about it.

*We're going to the Hollin's Cross chapel in the morning. It's been desecrated.*

*I won't be able to see you there, so please be careful.*

*Will there be someone there? Like the guy from the berry patch?*

*I don't know. Be careful, Evan. There will be nothing I can do if something bad happens.*

*Good night.*

*Good night, Evan.*

I sat on the porch a while longer, listening to the night, before I went up to bed.

We got up early and went down to find Mave already up and waiting with breakfast.

"I thought you could use a warm meal before you start off, my lovelies," she said.

"You're a lifesaver, Mave," I said, and meant it.

Mave blushed and waved us over to the table. We ate in silence, then got ready to leave.

"I'll have dinner ready when you get back," Mave said, with a quaver in her voice.

We smiled and waved before heading out.

As we left Hollin's Cross, I began to get excited and a bit nervous. We were heading to my first dungeon. There would be traps, and treasure, and probably a boss room. We might also find another chimera, or something worse.

The countryside on the way to the chapel looked perfectly normal. Lots of trees and grass. Houses in the distance. But when we came out of a stand of trees and could see the chapel, everything changed.

Almost nothing grew in the area around the chapel. The trees were dead and twisted. The little grass that remained was brown and sparse.

"There's no noise," said Halden.

"No birds, no insects, not even wind," said Nara.

From where we stood, I couldn't see any doors on the chapel, just a dark opening where they should have been. Even the early morning light couldn't get inside.

*Less like Dungeons & Dragons. More like Nightmare on Elm Street.*

"I'll need a little time to prepare," said Halden.

"Go ahead. Just let us know when you're ready," said Vagid.

I watched Halden prepare. He pulled out a necklace with a pendant on it and put it on. The pendant bore a sheaf of wheat, a flower, and the sun. Then he reached into his pack and took out a dark gray earthen bottle with a cork in it.

He saw me watching. "Holy oil. It's good to use if we have something like a lich or zombies."

Then he took out a glass bottle filled with a clear liquid. "Holy water, for most other undead things, at least the ones that have a body."

*A lich. An actual lich. Please don't let there be a lich.*

"Is the pendant special?" I asked.

"It is the symbol of Amaranth," was all Halden said.

"Ready," Halden said to Vagid.

We walked over to the opening. Up close, I could see a little way inside, but it was still eerie and quiet.

*Tired Guy?*

Nothing. Not even hold music.

Vagid took a calming breath, then stepped inside. We had to go in single file because of the debris in the entry. Halden went next, holy water in hand, then Nara, then me. Orrin took up the rear, an arrow already nocked.

*Fourth in line. In a horror movie, that's either the safest spot or the first to die.*

The layout of the chapel was identical to the Millbrook chapel.

"Here, ghosty, ghosty," I said. The whole group turned to look at me like I was a complete idiot.

Halden stopped for a moment, looking around. Then he touched his pendant and whispered a prayer. When he finished, we started searching. There were dark stains on the walls and floor.

As we explored, I noticed a small altar that had been turned over. A statue lay broken on the floor beside it. Both were covered in a dark stain. Halden approached the altar and righted it. The statue was too broken to put back together, but he picked up all the pieces and arranged them on the altar.

We moved on to the next room and found some rusted armor and scraps of clothing. In the room after that, we found pieces of bone, too broken to tell whether they were human or animal.

"Anyone know what this means?" asked Nara, pointing to some scratches on the wall.

It wasn't writing. It was a shape with eight sides, scratched deep into the stone. It looked familiar, but I just couldn't figure out why.

We'd covered the chapel proper without finding any sign of a ghost when Halden said, "Guess it's in the cellar."

*Cellar?*

"Lead the way," said Vagid.

Halden led us to a doorway, missing its door, with stairs going down.

"Let's light some torches," said Orrin.

We lit two torches, one for the front of the group and one for the rear.

The stairs seemed to go on for a really long time, but that was probably just my horror-movie sense going wild. We reached the bottom and stepped into a large, open room. Everything seemed quiet.

"Something is moving around down here," said Nara. Right then, the torch flames flickered.

"It feels really cold too," Orrin said.

We were grouped by the stairs, which came down in the middle of one wall. In the center of the room, a light suddenly appeared. It was shaped like a man, a man who'd been through a bad fight. He wore old-looking armor with large gashes and dents in it, and an open-faced helmet with a big dent in the side. That must have been what killed him. Then I saw the stab wounds. Twenty, maybe thirty. His shoes seemed different, but it was hard to tell what was off about them with the armor in the way.

He raised his fists to the ceiling and screamed, "How many times do I have to say it!"

Halden moved forward and started to pray loudly. The glowing man (*Mister Soul Glow?*) pointed at some debris and swept his hand toward Halden. The debris flew at him, knocking him to the ground.

"What is it saying?" yelled Nara.

"No idea," yelled Vagid.

*Huh? English? It's talking in English!*

Halden got back up and started praying loudly again. Mister Soul Glow tried the debris trick again, but Nara was able to shield Halden.

I stepped forward and held up both arms, the way people do in movies to show they're unarmed. Mister Soul Glow swept both his arms in my direction. I didn't see the rocks coming, but I felt them. I wasn't sure how it happened, but I was sitting down now, with a coppery taste in my mouth. Orrin dropped his torch, ran over to me, and yelled, "Evan! Let me take a look at you!"

I raised my hand to my forehead and felt something wet and sticky. I pulled my hand away and looked at it. *Huh. Who's bleeding?*

"That's a nasty wound," Orrin said, looking at me.

*Guess it's me.*

"Can't you just leave me alone? I'm trapped here, and no one understands me!" said Mister Soul Glow.

I tried to remember what I'd been doing a second ago. It was important, but my brain just wouldn't cooperate.

Halden was still praying loudly, and his pendant was starting to glow. Nara was still shielding him from the debris attacks. Vagid came over to help Orrin patch me up.

Mister Soul Glow saw Halden's pendant start glowing and changed his tactics. Instead of more debris attacks, he charged toward Halden and swung his arm, like he was slapping Halden's chest. The effect was immediate. Halden stopped praying as frost formed where Mister Soul Glow had touched him. His face went pale, and he sank to his knees, gasping.

*What was I doing? It was really important.*

"I wish I knew what it was saying," yelled Vagid.

My head was throbbing, but one thought came through clearly. *He's speaking English. If he can understand me, maybe he'll stop.*

"WAIT!" I screamed, as loud as I could, in English.

Everything stopped. My party all stared at me with their mouths open. Mister Soul Glow stared at me with his mouth open. Halden just stared at me.
