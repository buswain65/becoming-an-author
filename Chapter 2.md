# Chapter 2

I wasn't alone. Figures in long robes stood around the table, hoods pulled low, faces lost in shadow.

*I really hope they're hot girls*, I thought. *Otherwise this dream is taking a kinky turn.*

One of the figures stepped forward and raised a hand. It was a very hairy hand. *So much for hot girls.*

And the hand was holding something. Pliers. And not in a fun way.

"Hi there, Mister Pliers," I said. "I don't really need pliers right now. A bathroom, maybe?"

Another voice, cool and bored: "Not yet. First, the assessment." *Okay, Bored Dude.*

Mister Pliers stepped back. He did not put the pliers away.

Another hooded figure stepped forward holding some kind of crystal ball, and his hands weren't as hairy. *There's still hope for the hot babes!*

His voice put an end to that. "Appraise!"

*Appraise? Like in isekai? That's weird. And clearly a dude. Okay, Ball Guy.*

The crystal ball glowed briefly. Then Ball Guy said, "There is a problem."

"Well, I didn't get any time to study," I said.

"Silence, fool!" said Ball Guy.

"What kind of problem?" asked Bored Dude.

*The kind of problem where this dream is a **r**ea**l **sausage fest*, I thought sadly.

Ball Guy turned to Bored Dude. "He doesn't have any abilities. He's just..."

"A failure?" said Bored Dude.

*R**u**d**e**. **T**h**i**s i**s my d**r**e**a**m**.*

Ball Guy showed the crystal ball to Bored Dude.

"Do you have any idea what this summoning *cost*?" Bored Dude snapped at no one in particular. "This is unacceptable." *Wow. Bored Dude sounds pissed off.* "Find out what he knows! He must have done something to cause this fiasco!"

*Bored Dude needs to watch his blood pressure, or he'll have a stroke.*

"We will return to Lumenhold." He turned to Mister Pliers. "You remain here and find out why the ritual failed!"

*Looks like Ball Guy really screwed up.*

Then most of the robed figures turned and left. As I watched them go, I realized the room was more like a huge domed chamber, and there were a lot more people in it than I'd thought. Some wore armor and carried swords and pikes.

On their way out, they crossed a floor with some strange patterns on it. Or rather *in* it, since they looked like black arcs in the stone. *I wonder what caused those**?** **Almost like inlaid metal, except metal would shine.** *

One of the guards stood out from the rest: better armor, a cloak, and the kind of posture that comes from giving orders. *Fancy Guard. He must be important.* When the robed figures left, he stayed behind with the guards.

*This is what I get for talking about isekai at dinner. A really weird dream about it.*

Then Mister Pliers came closer and pushed back his hood.

*He's definitely not going to win any beauty contests. Not with those scars and missing teeth.*

Right then, my attention was yanked back to Mister Pliers. He was using his namesake on my left pinky.

The pain was white and enormous and completely real. Nobody has dreams that hurt like this. *Not a dream. Hooded guys, a ritual, Appraise. I've been summoned, like in the books. Except in the books, the hero gets a cheat skill, not Mister Pliers.*

"What the hell, dude!" I yelled.

"What did you do?" asked Mister Pliers.

"Do? About what?" I screamed, struggling to figure out what he meant.

Mister Pliers released my pinky, which now bent at a sad angle. He slowly moved the pliers to my ring finger.

"The ritual," he said coldly.

"I think we're really not on the same p— AAAAHHH!"

He broke my ring finger.

Mister Pliers stepped away from the table to get a drink of water. *Apparently even torturers need to hydrate.*

He came back and asked, "The ritual has been successful for hundreds of years. Until you came. You are the problem. So what did you do?"

I was at a complete loss. "I don't even know what ritual you're talking about!" I said, as he slowly moved the pliers to my middle finger.

*Typing was going to be a problem going forward.*

"Wait! Wait! Wa— AAAAAH!"

There went my middle finger. No more flipping off bad drivers with that hand.

"You must have done something to cause this!" said Mister Pliers, moving toward my pointer finger.

"Huh! Huh! Wa— I don— huh!" I struggled to come up with some kind of answer, but the pain made it impossible to think.

*Crack.*

There went my ability to point at things.

*I'd started the day with ten working fingers. I was down to six**.** **M**y** **t**y**p**i**ng **s**p**e**e**d** **w**a**s** **r**ea**lly go**ing to s**uffer**.*

I don't remember passing out, but I remember the ice-cold water waking me up.

My left hand throbbed in time with my heartbeat. I didn't see Mister Pliers right away, but I could hear someone shuffling around just outside the light.

"What did you do to the ritual?" Mister Pliers asked from somewhere in the dark.

"I was asleep! In a tent! I had pizza!" I screamed, hoping that would satisfy him.

"Pizza? Is that some kind of poison?" he asked, stepping back into the dim light around my table. "Answer me!" he yelled, and punched my left hand.

White-hot pain took my breath away.

I must have passed out again. Another splash of ice-cold water, and I was awake. *So much for the warm gear.*

Mister Pliers had swapped his pliers for a new toy: a thin, six-inch blade.

"Which kingdom sent you?" he asked.

*Should I rename him? Mister Knife? Mack the Knife?*

"North Carolina!" I yelled.

"There is no kingdom called North Carolina!" yelled Mister Pliers, slowly bringing the knife to rest on my right arm. Not like he was cutting a steak. Like he wanted to shave the hair off my arm.

Out of the corner of my eye, I saw Fancy Guard turn away and walk out.

I'm not going to tell you what came next. I'll just say I learned some new sounds I could make.

I must have blacked out again, because another bucket of water hit me, and I came awake screaming.

I don't remember much of that part. I refused to look at my arm. The pain was astounding; every breath of air across it burned. My whole body was shaking, from shock and from the cold water.

*At least I don't need the bathroom anymore.*

Mister Pliers held a glowing rod in front of my face. "Time for some heat," he said.

"How did you block the appraisal?" he asked, in an almost calm voice.

"I'm— huh— a— huh— developer—" I could barely get the words out.

New pain jolted me awake. Hot, blinding pain on my upper thigh. I screamed, or tried to, but I couldn't get enough breath for it.

Another bucket of water hit me. I licked my lips and tasted salt, and not from the bucket. My eyes were blurry. Small wonder, with this pain. I could feel several spots on my thighs pulsing in time with my heart.

Mister Pliers came back into view. "Now for my personal favorite," he said, showing me a sturdier, eight-inch knife. He pointed it at my calf. "Then what are you?"

"Nobody," I sputtered. "I'm nobody."

I watched what the knife did. I just didn't believe it was actually happening.

Another splash of ice water. Several more spots throbbed like my calf now. I had no idea why I was still alive, or still sane.

Mister Pliers walked over to a chair and sat down. *Oh, thank God. He's taking a break.*

"Get the clubs and start working on him," I heard him tell some guards.

*Great. I finally go clubbing, and I'm the one getting clubbed.*

I closed my eyes and hoped I would die before much longer. I could hear the thuds of the clubs, but I couldn't feel them anymore. *This must be what dying is like.*

"You're not dead," a voice said.

*Well, if I'm not dead, then what am I?*

"You are close to death, but I am keeping you alive," the soft voice said.

*Can't I just die? I'm nothing.*

"You are someone very important," said the voice. It sounded a little tired.
